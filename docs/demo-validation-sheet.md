tasks. Ask permission before recording notes or screen activity, and do not

## Notes and follow-up

- Where did the participant hesitate or choose an unexpected control?
- Which labels or state changes were unclear?
- Did any loading, empty, error, or recovery state leave them unsure?
- What did the participant expect to happen after refresh or Back/Forward?
- Accessibility observations (keyboard, focus, zoom, contrast, screen reader):
- Highest-impact change to test next:
- Follow-up owner and date:

Do not turn one walkthrough into a completion-rate claim. Summarize the number
and type of sessions, task outcomes, observed issues, and product revision only
after sessions occur. Capture screenshots separately during integrated visual
QA; do not present these prompts as user validation evidence.
